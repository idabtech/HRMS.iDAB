Full & Final Settlement — Operational Guide Section 1: Overview

1 System Overview & Purpose

The Full & Final Settlement & Clearance module provides a modern, paperless, and legally compliant way to manage employee offboarding. It eliminates scattered emails, paper forms, and disconnected spreadsheets.

# Capability What It Delivers
1 Financial Settlement Calculation Automatically computes gross earnings, deductions, and net payable amount in real time
2 Departmental & Asset Clearance Tracks equipment returns, credential revocations, and handover completions per department
3 Interactive Employee Clearance Form Employee checks items, writes handover notes, and updates progress from any device — no login required
4 Digital Sign-Off with Verification Captures legally valid digital signatures (drawn or uploaded) with date, time, and IP address
5 Custom Questions Per Section HR can attach custom fields (text, dropdowns, dates) to any section — filled by HR or by the employee online
6 Official PDF Export Generates a print-ready, executive PDF for payroll, legal archives, and audit records

2 End-to-End Workflow — Visual Overview

The settlement process has three main stages and a final confirmed outcome. Each stage is owned by a different party:

1
🧑‍💼 HR Admin Creates the Settlement Form
Select employee → Enter separation details → Set earnings & deductions → Build the departmental clearance checklist → Add optional custom questions → Save the record
2
📨 Secure Link is Shared with the Employee
Auto-email sent with a clean, encrypted link OR HR copies and shares the link manually via WhatsApp, Slack, or email — no login required for the employee
3
👤 Employee Reviews, Fills & Signs the Form
Opens link → Reviews financials → Checks off clearance items → Adds handover notes → Accepts legal declaration → Provides digital signature → Submits
✓
✅ Settlement Confirmed, Locked & PDF Ready
Employee sees confirmation certificate → HR reviews checklist & notes → PDF available for payroll, legal, and accounting archives

Full & Final Settlement — Operational Guide Section 4: HR Admin Guide

4 HR Admin: Creating the Settlement Form

1
Employee & Separation Details
Navigate to Full & Final Settlement in the sidebar and click Create Settlement. Select the employee — their Employee Code, Designation, Department, and Date of Joining are automatically filled. Enter the Last Working Day (LWD) and the Reason for Separation (e.g., Resignation Accepted, Contract Ended, Mutual Release, Retirement).
2
Financial Clearance Breakdown
Set all earnings (Salary, Leave Encashment, Gratuity, Bonuses) and deductions (Notice Recovery, Loan Balance, Asset Damages). The Net Final Payable (A - B) calculates automatically in real time — no manual math needed. Click Add Earning or Add Deduction for custom rows.
3
Departmental Clearance Checklist
Build checklist rows per department. Set the Category (any department name — IT, Admin, Finance, Operations, Facility, Legal, etc.) and the Checkpoint Item (specific thing to be returned or verified). Click Add Checkpoint for more rows. Click the trash icon to remove any row. There is no fixed template — build any checklist for any industry or organization type.
4
Custom Questions (Optional)
Each section has an Add Question button to attach custom fields. Set the question title, input type (Short Text, Paragraph, Dropdown, Date, Number), and who fills it: HR Certified (filled at creation by HR) or Employee to Fill Online (shown to the employee on the clearance form). Mark as Required if mandatory.
5
Save the Settlement
Click Save Full & Final Settlement. The system assigns a unique Reference Number (e.g., FNF-202609-0001), sets the status to Draft, and generates a secure encrypted share link valid for 30 days.

Financial Breakdown at a Glance

💰 Earnings & Payables (A)
  • Salary payable up to Last Working Day
  • Earned & unused leave encashment
  • Gratuity (if applicable)
  • Performance bonus or incentive
  • + Any custom earning row
💸 Deductions & Recoveries (B)
  • Notice period shortfall recovery
  • Outstanding loan or advance balance
  • Asset / hardware damage recovery
  • Training bond recovery
  • + Any custom deduction row
NET FINAL PAYABLE AMOUNT = A − B Auto-calculated ✓

6 Sharing the Form with the Employee

Every settlement has a secure, encrypted private token link — a long unique code that protects the form from unauthorized access without exposing internal database IDs.

📧 Option A — Auto Email
Open the settlement record and click Send to Employee. An official email is sent automatically with the employee's name, reference number, net payable amount, and a direct "Open Clearance Form" button. Status updates to Sent with share date logged.
🔗 Option B — Direct Link Sharing
Copy the secure link from the settlement page and share it via WhatsApp, Slack, email, or any messaging platform. The employee clicks the link and the clearance form opens immediately — no login required.
⏳ Link Validity
The secure link is valid for 30 days from creation. If the employee does not open the form before it expires, they will see a polite expiry notice. HR will need to regenerate a new link from the settlement management page.
Full & Final Settlement — Operational Guide Section 7: Employee Guide

7 Employee: Filling & Signing the Clearance Form

When the employee opens the secure link, they see an executive-styled digital clearance document. The form works on any device — desktop, tablet, or smartphone. No login is required.

Section A — Document Header & Financial Statement

The top of the form displays the company name, official settlement reference number, and the date issued. Below this is the complete financial breakdown:

  • Employee name, code, designation, department, date of joining
  • Last working day and reason for separation
  • Itemized earnings list and deductions list
  • Net Final Payable Amount prominently displayed

Section B — Interactive Departmental Clearance Checklist

This is the most interactive section. Here is exactly how it works:

↑ Demo: Showing how the interactive checklist looks. When an item is checked, it turns green, the category counter updates, and the progress bar animates.

⚡ Live Progress Updates
Every time the employee checks a box: the badge flips to green, the department counter (e.g., "1/2 Cleared") updates instantly, the top progress bar animates forward, and when all items in a category are done, the folder icon becomes a green checkmark.

Section C — Legal Declaration & Signature

C1
Additional Custom Questions (if any)
If HR added employee-targeted custom fields, they appear here — the employee fills in forwarding email, phone, repository links, or any other requested information.
C2
Legal Declaration Checkbox
The employee reads the formal undertaking confirming they have returned all property, revoked all access, and maintain NDA confidentiality. They must check "I have read, understood, and agree." — this is mandatory.
C3
General Comments (Optional)
A free-form text area for the employee to write their future contact email, phone number, forwarding address, or general handover notes.

Section D — Digital Signature

Two signature methods are available — the employee chooses whichever is more convenient:

✏️ Draw Signature
⬆️ Upload Image
Draw here using mouse, trackpad, or finger on touchscreen → [ Clear & Redraw anytime ]

Alternatively, switch to the Upload tab to drag-and-drop or select a PNG/JPG image of your signature. A preview is shown before submission.

Full & Final Settlement — Operational Guide Section 10: Suggestions & Improvements

10 Suggestions & Recommended Improvements

The following enhancements are recommended to make the Full & Final Settlement module more complete, legally robust, and user-friendly in a real production environment.

🔴 High Priority

#FeatureWhy It Matters
P1 Multi-Stage Countersign (Manager → HR) Currently only the employee signs. Most organizations require the manager and HR head to also countersign. A three-stage approval (Employee → Manager → HR) would make the document legally complete and compliant with standard HR practices.
P2 Email Notification to HR on Submission When the employee submits the form, HR receives no automatic alert. They have to manually check the dashboard. An automatic email/notification to HR on employee submission ensures timely awareness and faster processing.
P3 Link Regeneration Button If the 30-day token expires before the employee signs, HR has no easy way to generate a new link. A clear "Regenerate & Resend Link" button on the settlement detail page is essential for real-world usage.
P4 Token Expiry Visible on Dashboard HR should be able to see the link expiry date on the settlements list and detail page so they can proactively follow up before it lapses.

🟡 Medium Priority

#FeatureWhy It Matters
P5 One-Click WhatsApp Share Add a WhatsApp share button on the settlement page. Most HR teams communicate via WhatsApp and this eliminates manual copy-paste of links.
P6 Settlement Activity Log Record every status change (Draft → Sent → Signed → Cleared) with the actor name and timestamp. Provides a full audit trail for compliance and legal purposes.
P7 Bulk Excel/CSV Export Allow HR to export a filtered list of settlements to Excel for payroll batch processing, quarterly reporting, and analytics.
P8 HR Remarks per Checklist Item Currently only the employee adds remarks per item. HR should also be able to note internal verification comments (e.g., "Return physically verified by IT — 17 Sep") visible only in the admin view, not the employee form.
P9 Settlement Revision (Recall & Edit) If HR sends the form and then realizes an error in the financials, there is no way to revise it. A "Recall & Edit" feature would allow HR to withdraw, correct, and re-send without creating a duplicate record.
P10 Mobile Full-Screen Signature Canvas On small mobile screens, the signature drawing canvas is limited in size. A "full-screen mode" that expands the canvas on mobile would significantly improve the drawing experience on smartphones.

🟢 Future Roadmap

#FeatureWhy It Matters
P11 OTP Verification on Submission Before the employee can submit, send a one-time OTP to their registered mobile number to verify identity. Strengthens the legal standing of the digital signature.
P12 Auto-Calculated Statutory Amounts If employee salary and leave data already exists in HRMS, auto-populate Gratuity and Leave Encashment amounts into the earnings table, reducing manual entry and calculation errors.
P13 Department-Wise Clearance Approval Allow each department head (IT, Admin, Finance) to independently approve their specific checklist section from their own login, building multi-layer verification.
P14 HR Dashboard Widget A quick-glance widget on the main HRMS dashboard showing: X Settlements pending signature, Y signed this month, Z links expired without signing.
P15 Reminder Email Button Allow HR to click "Send Reminder" to resend the secure link email to an employee who has not completed the form within a configured number of days.
